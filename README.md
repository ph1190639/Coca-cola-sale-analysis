# Coca-Cola Sales Analysis using Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Coca-Cola sales data across **retailers, regions, beverage brands, and months** using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using data cleaning, Pivot Tables, and visualizations.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Understand sales performance by retailer, region, brand, and month
- Identify the highest- and lowest-performing segments
- Analyze monthly sales trends and seasonality
- Compare revenue and units sold across beverage brands
- Understand brand performance across regions and months
- Present findings through clear Excel visualizations

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The project started with reviewing and preparing the raw sales data.

Key steps included:

- Reviewing the dataset
- Cleaning and standardizing data
- Checking data consistency
- Formatting dates and numerical fields
- Organizing dimensions such as Month, Retailer, Region, and Beverage Brand
- Preparing the data for Pivot Table analysis

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Monthly Sales Analysis

Analyzed total sales by month to understand overall sales trends and seasonality.

**Key finding:**
- Total sales were approximately **$8.68 million**
- Sales were relatively lower during the early months
- Sales increased significantly during the middle of the year
- July and December were among the strongest sales periods

### 2. Sales by Retailer

| Retailer | Sales Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Sodapop | 50.7% |
| Fizzysip | 29.8% |
| BevCo | 14.9% |
| DreamCo | 4.7% |

**Insight:** Sodapop was the largest retailer, while Sodapop and Fizzysip together contributed more than **80% of total sales**.

### 3. Sales by Region

| Region | Sales Contribution |
|---|---:|
| West | 32.6% |
| Northeast | 20.6% |
| Southeast | 18.6% |
| South | 14.9% |
| Midwest | 13.2% |

**Insight:** The **West** was the strongest-performing region, while the Midwest had the lowest contribution.

### 4. Sales by Beverage Brand

| Brand | Sales Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Coca-Cola | 23.2% |
| Dasani Water | 19.9% |
| Diet Coke | 17.1% |
| Sprite | 14.2% |
| Powerade | 13.7% |
| Fanta | 11.9% |

**Insight:** **Coca-Cola** was the highest-performing beverage brand by sales.

### 5. Units Sold by Brand

| Brand | Units Sold Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Coca-Cola | 24.1% |
| Dasani Water | 17.5% |
| Diet Coke | 17.5% |
| Sprite | 15.8% |
| Fanta | 12.8% |
| Powerade | 12.4% |

This comparison helped evaluate product performance based on sales volume as well as revenue.

### 6. Brand Performance by Month

Analyzed individual beverage brands across different months to identify:

- Seasonal movements
- Strong and weak months
- Differences in brand performance over time

### 7. Brand Performance by Region

Compared beverage brands across regions to understand how product performance varied geographically.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. Total sales were approximately **$8.68M**.
2. **Sodapop** was the largest retailer, contributing approximately **50.7%** of total sales.
3. The top two retailers contributed more than **80%** of overall sales.
4. The **West** was the strongest region, contributing approximately **32.6%**.
5. **Coca-Cola** was the leading beverage brand by sales and units.
6. Sales showed noticeable **seasonal variation**, with strong performance around July and December.
7. Brand-level monthly and regional analysis can help identify opportunities for targeted promotions and inventory planning.
